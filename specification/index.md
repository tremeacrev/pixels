# Pixels

Pixels is a terminal art program inspired by Electric Sheep. It presents a continuously evolving
composition of abstract shapes and color, combining flowing motion with energetic transformations.
Users view it remotely over SSH. The only interaction with the running program is Ctrl-C.

- [Artistic direction](artistic-direction.md): the storm's vision, visual language, and rhythm.
- [Running](running.md): starting the program, its command line, and its streams.
- [Keyboard and signals](keyboard-and-signals.md): ignored keys, Ctrl-C, and signals.
- [Composition](composition.md): the evolving artwork and its use of the terminal area.
- [Terminal state](terminal-state.md): drawing on the screen and restoring the terminal.
- [Environment](environment.md): rendering backends, terminal capabilities, and timeouts.
