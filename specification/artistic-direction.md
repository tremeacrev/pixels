# Artistic direction

Create a magnificent celestial storm of impossible light. Evoke awe through overwhelming
immensity and power, sharpened by a touch of beauty; discovery serves that impression.

Keep the imagery radically abstract. Let light, form, and space follow their own visual logic,
free of physical plausibility or literal astronomy.

Express this direction within the [terminal's capabilities](environment.md) and
[available area](composition.md), preserving its priorities as color and detail are reduced.

Make waves and sheets of light the dominant substance. Plasma and electrical currents share a
supporting role with torrents of particles and debris; luminous clouds lend occasional atmosphere.

Vary the dominant forms over time: sweeping sculptural folds, sharp fractured planes, and
intricate filamented surfaces all belong to the same storm.

Keep the composition open and monumental. Give enormous structures room to command the view,
using depth and secondary layers to suggest further immensity without crowding the scene.
Avoid an overall rhythm of expansion and contraction.

Move between scales, connecting vast interacting structures with their turbulent interiors.
Favor expansive perspectives over engulfing ones, making immersion an occasional intensification.

Make motion violent and turbulent, with force gathering and breaking into powerful releases.
Convey magnitude through scale and depth more than slowness. Sustain charged energy punctuated
by distinct climaxes, allowing only brief lulls for tension and contrast.

Let temporary order emerge from asymmetric turbulence. Vast alignments, symmetries, or repetitions
briefly hold, then fracture or dissolve; sustained order is rare.

Lead with electric blues, violets, cyan, and magenta. Incandescent whites, golds, amber, and reds
intensify the storm; prismatic color adds occasional richness. Use stark, sparsely colored
treatments most sparingly.

Evolve form, color, and motion coherently, carrying the storm's identity through its
transformations.

Exclude imagery resembling microscopic biology, obvious loops, persistent kaleidoscope patterns,
recognizable objects, cartoon effects, excessive visual clutter, rapid flashing, and transitions
that feel like switching between unrelated presets.
